# Beautiful Bleep for musicians

Beautiful Bleep is a modular sound engine that runs as a plugin in Ableton Live,
as a standalone app and in your browser. Play a ready-made instrument or effect,
or patch your own with cables between small building blocks. Every knob is a
modulation destination.

## Three ideas to know

- **One engine, three places.** Plugin, app and browser run the same board and
  modules, and a project made in one opens in the others.
- **Cables everywhere.** Drag a cable onto any knob to modulate it. See
  [Modulation](learn/modulation.md).
- **Play first, patch later.** Presets work without a cable. When you want more,
  open the preset as a cell and change it. See [Cells and panels](learn/cells-and-panels.md).
