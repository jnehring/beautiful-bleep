# Your first sound

You do not need to patch anything to make a sound. Bleep Instrument and Bleep FX
are ready-made patches with a front panel of knobs and sliders.

## An instrument

1. Put **Bleep Instrument** on a MIDI track in Live.
2. Pick a preset, for example *Warm Saws*.
3. Play it with MIDI.

## An effect

1. Put **Bleep FX** on an audio track.
2. Pick a preset, for example *Dub Ping-Pong*.
3. Play audio through it.

Both open on the preset's **panel**, not on the board. Turn the knobs; that is all
there is to it.

## When you want more

An instrument or effect preset is a small [cell](../learn/cells-and-panels.md) on
the same board as everything else. Press **Open Board** and you see it there.
You can open the cell, change what is inside, cable it to other parts and save it
under your own name. Factory presets are never overwritten.

Cables between several plugins need **Bleep Board** in the set, because the board
is saved there. [Your first patch](../learn/first-patch.md) walks through it.

Instruments and effects are planned for the Bleep App and Bleep Play; today
they are Live plugins. [Ways to use Bleep](ways-to-use.md) has the table.
