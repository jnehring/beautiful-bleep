Score the assistant's reply to a request to change or explain a patch.
5: does exactly what was asked with minimal, tidy changes; the explanation says what changed in one or two clear sentences; no invented features; admits limits.
4: right result, slightly verbose or one small untidy choice.
3: works but changes more than asked, or explanation is unclear.
2: partial or confusing; claims things that did not happen.
1: wrong or misleading.
