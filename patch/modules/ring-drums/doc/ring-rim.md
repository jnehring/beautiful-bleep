# Ring Rim

## Introduction

Short, high, woody clicks: a rim shot (the stick hitting the rim of a
snare) or a clave (two hardwood sticks).

It belongs to the **Ring** drums: deep, round voices built from struck
resonators, noise and detuned square waves.

## How does it work?

*Rim* strikes two high resonators at once and passes them through a
high-pass and a little saturation, for a sharp crack. *Clave* is one
resonator, higher and purer, that rings a little longer. *Decay* multiplies
each voice's own ringing time, from a quarter to four times as long.

**Playing it.** A rising edge at *Trig* plays the voice. *Velocity*, when
patched, is read at that moment: 100 % is full volume, 50 % half volume.
Unpatched, every hit is at full velocity. Velocity changes only the loudness,
never the sound, so soft hits keep their character. Hitting again while the
voice still sounds adds to the ringing.

## How to use it?

- **Rim on the offbeats:** a light, ticking pulse that leaves room for a
  snare.
- **Clave pattern:** *Voice* Clave, on a syncopated pattern.
- **Wood block:** *Clave* tuned down 7 – 12 semitones, *Decay* around 2×.

## Reference

### Knobs and settings

| Control | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | Rim, Clave | Rim |
| Tune | −12 … +12 st | 0 st |
| Decay | 0.25 – 4 × | 1 × |
| Level | 0 – 100 % | 80 % |

- **Voice:** which sound plays. A setting, not a knob.
- **Tune:** shifts the whole voice up or down, in semitones.
- **Decay:** multiplies how long the voice rings.
- **Level:** the output volume.

*Tune*, *Decay* and *Level* are modulation destinations.

### Inputs

| Input | Signal | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Trig | trigger | a rising edge plays the voice (gates work too) |
| Velocity | control, 0 … 1 | the loudness of each hit, read at the trigger; unpatched = 100 % |

### Outputs

| Output | Signal | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Out | audio | the drum |
