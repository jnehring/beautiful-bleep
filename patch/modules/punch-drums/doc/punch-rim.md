# Punch Rim

## Introduction

A rim shot: a hard, bright crack, shorter and more metallic than Ring
Rim.

It belongs to the **Punch** drums: tight, hard voices built from swept sine
waves, noise and crunchy metal.

## How does it work?

Three high resonators are struck at once, together with a tiny burst of
noise. A high-pass and saturation turn them into a sharp crack. *Decay*
multiplies how long they ring.

**Playing it.** A rising edge at *Trig* plays the voice. *Velocity*, when
patched, is read at that moment: 100 % is full volume, 50 % half volume.
Unpatched, every hit is at full velocity. Velocity changes only the loudness,
never the sound, so soft hits keep their character. Hitting again while the
voice still sounds adds to the ringing.

## How to use it?

- **Rim accents:** sparse hits between snare and hats.
- **Stick click:** *Decay* at 0.25 ×, *Tune* up.

## Reference

### Knobs and settings

| Control | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Tune | −12 … +12 st | 0 st |
| Decay | 0.25 – 4 × | 1 × |
| Level | 0 – 100 % | 80 % |

- **Tune:** shifts the whole voice up or down, in semitones.
- **Decay:** multiplies how long the voice rings.
- **Level:** the output volume.

*Tune*, *Decay* and *Level* are modulation destinations.

### Inputs

| Input | Signal | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Trig | trigger | a rising edge plays the voice (gates work too) |
| Velocity | control, 0 … 1 | the loudness of each hit, read at the trigger; unpatched = 100 % |

### Outputs

| Output | Signal | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Out | audio | the drum |
